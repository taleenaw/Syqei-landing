Partner with Syqei

Empower young technologists.

Syqei Studio is an emerging EdTech company working at the intersection of creative technology, ecology and education. Delivered through schools and youth organisations, our applied projects help develop the next generation of creative technologists.

Talk to us See the ways to partner

The hardest experience for a young person to get is real-world experience. Syqei combines project-based learning, applied technology and research translation so young people can work from problems that exist beyond the classroom.

Who we work with

People who can bring young people, a problem, or the means to reach them.

Every project starts with a real-world brief. We work with organisations that can host a cohort, fund access or contribute ecological research, field data and environmental context.

  • Schools and youth organisations with a cohort and a timetable
  • Sponsors funding access, scholarships and places
  • Universities with scientists, mentors, datasets and venues
  • Environmental and conservation organisations with a real brief
  • Government, councils and funders

If you can bring young people, a problem, or a way to reach them, we would love to talk.

Ways to partner

A way to partner for every kind of organisation.

Bring a cohort, contribute a real-world brief or fund access. Partnerships can support technology enrichment, research translation or widening participation.

Schools and youth orgs

Bring a Syqei project to your young people. We provide the project brief, learning design, ecological context, applied technology experience and facilitation. You provide the cohort.

By enquiry

Researchers and environmental orgs

We translate ecological research, field data and conservation challenges into accessible technology briefs for young technologists. The commitment stays light: no ongoing mentoring and no deliverable expected from your team.

Project partner

Sponsors

Fund digital inclusion and widening participation for young people who would not otherwise get access. Back scholarship places, a girls in tech or regional cohort, or a project presented with your brand.

By enquiry

Universities

Connect ecological research, datasets, venues and pathways into what comes next. University students can join as paid Syqei Fellows and help deliver project-based learning experiences.
