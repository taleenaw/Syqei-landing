For organisations

Help more people fall in love with nature.

People protect what they feel connected to. Syqei helps organisations inspire the next generation to discover, experience and care for the living world.

Who we're looking for

Places that help people experience nature.

We're looking to partner with a small number of organisations across Western Australia, including:

  • National parks & reserves
  • Botanic gardens
  • Wildlife parks & sanctuaries
  • Tourism organisations
  • Local councils
  • Conservation groups
  • Researchers & universities

If your organisation helps people experience nature, we'd love to hear from you.
