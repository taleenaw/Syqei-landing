What you get with every mission.

For students

  • A mission built from a real research problem
  • Hands-on tools like computer vision, 3D and code

  • The careers behind the mission and what they can now do
  • Read aloud, keyboard support and an optional coding path

For teachers

  • A printable guide with a 60 minute run sheet
  • Learning outcomes, discussion questions and an answer guide
  • Extensions for fast finishers or a second lesson
  • One class link. No logins or accounts to set up
  • Setup support from us for founding schools

How a founding pilot runs.

  1. Pick your classes

    Choose one or two classes from Year 9 to Year 12 and a lesson that suits you.

  2. We set you up

    You get the teacher guide, the class link and a quick device check for your laptops.

  3. Students run the mission

    Your teacher runs the lesson. We can be there for the first one if you're in Perth.

  4. You get the results

    A short completion summary and what your students said about it.

The practical details.

Time

Each mission fits a 50 to 60 minute lesson. Optional coding levels stretch it to a second lesson.

Devices

Runs in a modern browser on school laptops and Chromebooks. A lite mode switches off 3D for older devices. Nothing to install.

Privacy

No student accounts, names or emails. Progress saves on the student's own device and nothing they make is uploaded.

Where it fits

Digital Technologies, Science, STEM electives and careers programs. It also works as an enrichment session.

Accessibility

Read aloud on every step, full keyboard support, captions on video and short plain language instructions.

Ages

Written for students aged 14 to 18. Respectful, not childish. No points, badges or leaderboards.

Run a program outside school?

Holiday programs, tutoring centres and after-school clubs can license missions for their groups. You run the session. We provide the mission, the facilitator guide and a completion file for every participant.
