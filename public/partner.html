Partner with Syqei

Back young people building real technology.

Syqei is a creative technology studio for 14 to 18 year olds, delivered through schools and youth organisations. You can help shape a generation who tackles modern problems affecting the natural world around us.

Talk to us See the ways to partner

The hardest experience for a young person to get is real experience. You can be the reason they get it, by bringing Syqei Studio to your students, funding access, or sharing a real problem for them to build from.

Who we work with

People who can bring young people, a problem, or the means to reach them.

Every project starts with a real problem and someone who cares how it turns out. We work with organisations who can host a cohort, fund access, or bring a problem and the data behind it.

  • Schools and youth organisations with a cohort and a timetable
  • Sponsors funding access, scholarships and places
  • Universities with scientists, mentors, datasets and venues
  • Environmental and conservation organisations with a real brief
  • Government, councils and funders

If you can bring young people, a problem, or a way to reach them, we would love to talk.

Ways to partner

A way to partner for every kind of organisation.

Bring young people, share a real problem, or fund access. There is a way in whether you have a cohort, a brief or a budget.

Schools and youth orgs

Bring a Syqei project to your students. It runs with your cohort, on your timetable, with an expert and real data. Enrichment, extension, careers, girls in tech or a holiday program.

Why partner with us

Real experience for young people, real impact for you.

Reach the young people you care about

Get in front of a generation early, through something they actually want to be part of. Youth opportunity, girls in tech, regional reach and digital skills, not a recruitment pipeline.

Your work, turned into inspiration

Share a real problem and real data, and we turn it into something young people build from. You are giving access and context, not outsourcing a deliverable.

Impact you can stand behind

Young people getting real experience through technology for the living world. Widening participation, regional reach and outcomes that are easy to report on.

What guides us

Real problems, not exercises.

Every project is a genuine problem with real data. That is the whole point, for the young people building and for the partners who make it possible.

Never charge someone just to belong.

The community, the opportunities and the discovery stay free for young people. Organisations fund the parts that are genuinely scarce, like expert time, structured projects and real access.

Safeguarding comes first.

These are young people, some under 18. Child safety, consent and duty of care are built in from day one, never added on afterwards.
