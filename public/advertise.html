Advertising young people can trust

Every paid partnership is clearly labelled. Sponsors never receive participant or newsletter data.

We do not accept advertising for gambling, vaping, alcohol, high-caffeine products, harmful body-image products or anything inappropriate for young people. Every partnership must support the safety, curiosity and future opportunities of the Syqei community.
