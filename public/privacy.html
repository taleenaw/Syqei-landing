Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

Syqei is in its early stage, and this website is a way to share what we are building and to let people register interest in our first projects. This policy covers what we collect through this website today. It will be expanded before any projects run, since taking part in a project involves more information and consent, especially for participants under 18.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Syqei ("Syqei," "we," "our," "us") collects, uses, and discloses information obtained through our website located at syqei.com (the "Site"). By visiting the Site and registering your interest, you are agreeing to the terms of this Policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Policy, please contact us at hello@syqei.com.

1. What Information We Collect

Registering interest. If you register your interest through the Site, we collect the email address you provide and, if you choose to share it, the option you select describing what brings you here, for example that you would like to join a project, or that you represent an organisation. That is currently the only information this Site collects directly from you.

Basic Site usage information. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically logs standard technical information such as browser type, general device information, and approximate location derived from IP address, for security and performance purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you about Syqei's first projects and when spots or opportunities become available
  • To follow up with organisations who want to bring, fund, or partner on a project
  • To understand how many people are interested in Syqei, in aggregate. We do not build individual profiles from this Site's data

We do not sell your email address, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Submissions are stored and processed through Netlify's form handling service. We take reasonable technical measures to protect this information, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100 percent secure.

4. Your Choices

You can ask us to remove your details at any time by emailing hello@syqei.com. We will action this within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Young People & Safeguarding

Syqei is built for young people, including some who are under 18, and keeping them safe is foundational to everything we do. This Site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information here. If you are under 18 and would like to register interest, please do so with a parent or guardian's knowledge and involvement.

Many young people take part through their school or another organisation, who arrange it alongside a parent or guardian. Taking part in an actual project is separate from registering interest on this Site. Before any young person joins a project, we will put appropriate safeguarding measures in place, including parental or guardian consent for minors, and clear policies on supervision, conduct and data, and this Policy will be updated to reflect them. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with information through this Site, please contact us and we will remove it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as Syqei evolves, including a fuller update before projects begin. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this Policy? Email us at hello@syqei.com.
