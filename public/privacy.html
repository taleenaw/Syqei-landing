Privacy policy.

Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy covers what Syqei collects through this website at syqei.com. Our mission app, Syqei Studio, has its own short privacy notice for students. It does not use accounts, and student work stays on the student's device.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Syqei ("Syqei", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses and discloses information obtained through our website located at syqei.com (the "Site"). If you have any questions about this Policy, please contact us at hello@syqei.com.

1. What information we collect

Enquiries. If you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, the option you choose describing your role, and, if you choose to share them, your school or organisation and your message. If you tick the box, we also record that you would like updates about new missions.

Updates list. If you join our updates list, we collect the email address you provide.

Basic site usage information. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically logs standard technical information such as browser type, general device information and approximate location derived from IP address, for security and performance purposes.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use this Site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors are located. This is collected in aggregate. We have turned off Google Signals and advertising personalisation, and we do not use analytics to identify individual visitors. Google processes this information on our behalf, and you can read how at policies.google.com/privacy. Most browsers let you block analytics scripts if you prefer.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange pilots, licences or partnerships
  • To send mission updates if you asked for them
  • To understand interest in Syqei in aggregate. We do not build individual profiles from this Site's data

We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for advertising or share them with third parties for marketing.

3. How we store and protect your information

Submissions are stored and processed through Netlify's form handling service. We take reasonable technical measures to protect this information, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100 percent secure.

4. Your choices

You can ask us to update or remove your details at any time by emailing hello@syqei.com. We will action this within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Young people and safeguarding

Syqei missions are designed for secondary students aged 14 to 18. This website is mainly for teachers, schools, program providers, researchers and parents.

If you are under 15, please ask a parent or carer to contact us with you. We do not ask for a student's school, home address, exact location, date of birth or photographs through this website.

Students usually take part through their school or another organisation. Before a mission runs with young people, we agree supervision, consent and data arrangements with that school or organisation. If you believe a child under 15 has given us information through this Site, please contact us and we will remove it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as Syqei grows. Material changes will be shown with an updated date at the top of this page.

7. Contact us

Questions about this Policy? Email us at hello@syqei.com.
