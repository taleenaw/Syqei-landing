Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Taleena.

How it started

On paper, I may not be who you would expect to build a nature brand.

I study Computer Science and Cybersecurity, and I genuinely love creating technology. But the more time I spent behind a screen, the more I noticed how much I missed being outside.

Growing up in regional Western Australia, I was surrounded by extraordinary nature without really knowing how to experience it. Wildflowers would bloom along roads I had travelled countless times. Whales would pass the coast for weeks. Fungi would appear after rain and disappear before I knew to look.

Meanwhile, the technology around me was becoming better and better at keeping my attention. Every app gave me another reason to keep looking down. Very few gave me a reason to look up.

That led me to a simple question:

What if technology could help us reconnect with nature instead of distracting us from it?

What we're building

Syqei is a Western Australian nature brand designed to help people notice, understand and experience more of the living world.

Through seasonal storytelling, scientist conversations, real-world experiences and a free discovery tool, we're making it easier to find out what is happening in nature while there is still time to experience it.

Our goal is not to replace nature with technology. It is to create technology that points back outside.

Why "Syqei"?

Syqei is pronounced psy-key.

The name is inspired by Psyche, an Ancient Greek word associated with both the soul and the butterfly. Butterflies have long represented curiosity, change and transformation.

For Syqei, they are a reminder that even a small encounter with the natural world can change how we see a place, and our relationship with it.

Making it happen

Alongside my studies, a lot of my energy goes into getting more young women into STEM. I've mentored high school and primary school students across metro and regional WA, and interstate, and spoken on panels for events like International Women's Day, sharing what a path into tech and science can actually look like.

It's the same thread that runs through Syqei: helping people see a place for themselves in something they might have assumed wasn't for them, whether that's a career in science or simply noticing the nature already around them.

It seems to me that the natural world is the greatest source of excitement, the greatest source of visual beauty, the greatest source of intellectual interest.

— Sir David Attenborough
