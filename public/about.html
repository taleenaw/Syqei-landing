Hi, I'm Taleena.

Founder of Syqei. Computer Science and Cybersecurity student at UWA. Mentor to high school students across Western Australia.

How it started

I have loved building technology since I was at school. But the parts of tech that stayed with me were never the tutorials. They were the moments when I got to use real tools on a real problem and see where those skills could lead.

I grew up in regional Western Australia. For a lot of students there, and everywhere, it is hard to find an experience that connects technology to something bigger than another assignment on a screen.

Meanwhile, some of the most exciting technology in the world is being used by researchers down the road. Computer vision, 3D, data and AI, used to answer questions nobody has answered yet.

What we're building

Syqei turns that research into technology missions for secondary schools. Each mission takes students inside a real research problem, puts the tools in their hands, and ends with something they made and the careers behind it.

Missions run in one lesson, in the browser, with no logins. Every one comes with a teacher guide, so any teacher can run it without a tech background.

Our first mission, The Illusion Engine, is built from UWA research into how camouflaged moths fool birds, people and computers.

Why Syqei

Syqei is pronounced psy-key. The name comes from Psyche, an Ancient Greek word tied to both the soul and the butterfly. Butterflies have always stood for curiosity, change and transformation.

That is the whole idea. The student who starts a mission unsure of themselves is rarely the same person who finishes it.

Making it happen

Alongside my studies, a lot of my energy goes into helping more students see a place for themselves in technology. I have mentored high school and primary school students across metro and regional WA and interstate, and spoken on panels about what a path into tech and science can actually look like.

It is the same thread that runs through Syqei. Real research, real tools, and proof that you can build something meaningful.
