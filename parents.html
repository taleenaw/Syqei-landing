For parents and carers

What your teenager did with Syqei.

Syqei makes technology missions for secondary schools. In one lesson, students use real computer vision on a real research question, then see the careers that use it.

In Mission 001, students:

  1. Race a computer

    They hunt for camouflaged moths, then watch a computer vision model try the same pictures.

  2. Take apart an illusion

    They spin a moth wing in 3D and find out it's flat. The curve is painted on.

  3. Make their own

    They design an illusion that fools a depth estimator, the same kind of tool researchers used.

  4. Meet the jobs

    They see four careers behind the mission, and only one of them is mostly code.

Is it safe?

  • No accounts, names or email addresses. Students just open a link.
  • No ads, and no tracking inside the student app.
  • What students make stays on their own device unless they choose to download it.

Read how we keep students safe or the student app privacy notice.

Try it at home.

The full mission works in any modern browser. Ask your teenager to show you how they beat the computer, or how the computer beat them.
