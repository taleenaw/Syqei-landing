Trust and child safety.

What a principal, business manager or IT lead needs to know before booking. In plain language.

No student data

Students don't create accounts, log in or give their name or email. Progress saves in their own browser. Nothing they make is uploaded to us.

No ads, no tracking of students

The mission app has no ads and no advertising trackers. We never sell or share information.

Works on locked-down devices

Runs in a modern browser with nothing to install. A device check confirms it works before class.

People in your classroom

Anyone from Syqei who supports a lesson in person holds a current WA Working with Children Check, and your staff stay in charge of the room.

Child safe by design

We follow the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations. No chat, no public profiles, no student photos without written consent.

Insurance

Public liability insurance for in-person sessions. We'll send the certificate of currency with your booking.

ST4S

Safer Technologies 4 Schools is the national privacy and security assessment for school software. We're working through the ST4S Readiness Check for the mission app.

Because students don't log in and we don't collect student data, there's very little personal information to protect. We'll publish our result here.

Where things live

  • This website is hosted on Netlify.
  • The mission app doesn't store anything about students on our servers.
  • Student progress stays on the student's device.
  • Pilot requests are stored in Netlify Forms and only used to reply.
  • Website analytics use Google Analytics, with Signals and ad personalisation off. See our privacy policy.

If something goes wrong

Tell us at hello@syqei.com and we'll respond within one business day. If it involves a student's safety, follow your school's own child protection procedures first, then let us know.
