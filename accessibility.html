Accessibility.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA on this website and in our missions.

In the missions

  • Read aloud on every step.
  • Every step can be completed with a keyboard.
  • A non-3D version of the 3D activity, used automatically on devices without 3D graphics or in lite mode.
  • Captions on the researcher video.
  • Short, plain language instructions and no time pressure outside the optional moth race.
  • Reduced motion settings are respected.

On this website

  • A skip link, visible keyboard focus and labelled form fields.
  • Text alternatives for images.
  • Reduced motion settings are respected.

Known limits

  • The 30 second preview and the moth race need a pointer or touch to click on the moth. Keyboard users can use "Show me where it is" and still run the computer race.
  • We haven't had an independent accessibility audit yet.
