Pilot results.

What happened when 144 Year 9 students from six WA schools tried Syqei between September and October 2026. Every number below shows which session it came from and how many students it counts.

The pilots at a glance.

Students

144 Year 9 students who came to UWA for outreach days.

Schools

Lynwood SHS, Cecil Andrews College, Kiara College, Rockingham SHS, Pinjarra SHS and Ballajura Community College.

Sessions

24 September, a full 45 minute Syqei session. 27 October, half Syqei and half a startup workshop. 30 October, a 10 minute moth activity and a short talk.

Method

Anonymous paper surveys, typed up by hand from phone scans. A few unreadable answers are left out.

Teachers

8 teachers across the two October sessions answered a short survey about value, fit and barriers.

What this is

Early pilot feedback from small groups. It is not a controlled study, and we don't claim it is one.

What students and teachers told us.

86 of 93
students who rated the Syqei activity liked or loved it. 48 of 51 on 24 September and 38 of 42 on 30 October. The 27 October rating covered the whole session, so we left it out.
3 to 31
of 51 students said they knew quite a lot or a lot about tech careers, before and after one session. 24 September only.
18 of 18
students who weren't into tech, or couldn't see themselves working in it, still liked or loved it. 9 on 24 September and 9 on 30 October.
7 of 7
teachers who wrote about the session described students as engaged. 27 and 30 October. The 27 October notes cover the whole session.
4 of 4
teachers on 27 October placed Syqei in extension or gifted programs. None placed it under Science.
Numbers
On 27 and 30 October, the idea students wrote down without being asked was that a computer reads a picture as numbers.
