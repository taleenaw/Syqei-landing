SQ-LOG-0324 September 2026

Our first pilot

52 Year 9 students from two Perth schools. 48 of 51 liked or loved it. One school was cooler than the other, and one student said it could be more interactive. That was fair, so Mission 001 is now hands-on from the first minute.
