How Syqei works.

Every investigation follows the same loop, inside one 50 to 60 minute lesson.

  1. 1

    Discover

    A mystery appears.

  2. 2

    Make

    You change or create something.

  3. 3

    Test

    A real tool responds to what you made.

  4. 4

    Improve

    You try again based on what happened.

  5. 5

    Keep

    Your Field File records the experiment.

In The Illusion Engine, students hunt camouflaged moths, design a flat wing that looks curved, test it against a depth estimator, change it until it fools the machine and save the result.
