There is more than one way into tech.

Maybe you are into coding, design, games, AI, data or just making things. Syqei Studio brings young people with different interests and ideas together to explore technology through real projects.

I always knew I loved technology, but I wanted to do more with it than follow tutorials and tick off another course.

Hi! I'm Taleena. I study Computer Science and Cybersecurity, and I've always been the kind of person who gets excited by what technology can make possible.

The parts of tech that stay with you aren't usually the tutorials. They're the moments where you get to try ideas, work with other people and see where those skills could lead. For a lot of young people, it can be hard to find opportunities that connect technology to something bigger than another assignment or project on a screen. Growing up in Western Australia, surrounded by incredible wildlife and landscapes, made me wonder why tech and nature are so often treated as separate worlds. Syqei came from wanting to bring them together by giving young people the chance to use technology to tackle problems affecting the natural world.

There has to be a better way to start.
