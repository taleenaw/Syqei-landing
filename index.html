What Syqei is

Discover

Seasonal stories about what's blooming, migrating and emerging across Western Australia, with real places to go and see it.

Understand

The founder

Hi, I'm Taleena.

I'm a Computer Science student at UWA who kept noticing how much extraordinary nature was passing me by, right here in Western Australia. Syqei is my way of helping people notice it too, and get outside while it's still happening.

The people behind Syqei

Nature, explained by the people who know it best.

Syqei works with scientists, researchers, photographers and local organisations to turn specialist knowledge into experiences anyone can enjoy. We're building close to Western Australia's research community, so the wonder always sits on real expertise, never guesswork.
