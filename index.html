How one mission works.

Every mission follows the same four moves, so teachers know what to expect and students know where they are.

  1. Meet the research

    Each mission starts with a real question from working scientists. Students hear it from them.

  2. Use the tools

    Students use tools and techniques from research. Computer vision, 3D, data and code, all in the browser.

  3. Make something

    They design their own solution, test it and save it to a field file they can download.

  4. See the careers

    Every mission ends with what students can now do and the jobs that use these tools for real.

hi, that's me

I always knew I loved technology, but I wanted to do more with it than follow tutorials and tick off another course.

Hi! I'm Taleena. I study Computer Science and Cybersecurity, and I've always been the kind of person who gets excited by what technology can make possible.

The parts of tech that stay with you aren't usually the tutorials. They're the moments where you get to try ideas, work with other people and see where those skills could lead. For a lot of young people, it can be hard to find opportunities that connect technology to something bigger than another assignment or project on a screen. Growing up in Western Australia, surrounded by incredible wildlife and landscapes, made me wonder why technology and ecology are so often treated as separate worlds. Syqei came from wanting to bring them together by giving young people the chance to explore applied technology through problems affecting the natural world.

There has to be a better way to start.

Questions schools ask.

Do students need accounts or emails?

No. Students open one class link. There are no logins, and progress saves in the browser on their own device.

What devices does it work on?

Recent Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on laptops and Chromebooks. Phones and tablets work but a laptop is more comfortable. Open the device check on one student laptop to be sure.

Where does it fit in the curriculum?

It suits Digital Technologies and Science in Years 9 and 10, STEM electives and careers programs. See where it fits. We can map it to your program on request.

What data do you collect about students?

No names, emails or accounts. Nothing students make is uploaded. Read the student app privacy notice.

Can a teacher run it without you?

Yes. That's the point. The teacher guide has a minute-by-minute run sheet, questions to ask and an answer guide. Founding schools in Perth can also have us there for the first lesson.

How does payment work?

We agree a fixed quote first, then send one invoice. School purchase orders are fine.

What if it doesn't work on the day?

If something on our side stops the lesson running, we reschedule it at no cost.

Do you work with programs outside school?

Yes. Holiday programs, tutoring centres and clubs can license missions for their groups. Tell us about your program.
