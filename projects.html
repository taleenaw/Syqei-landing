Try this with your eyes

Is this wing curved?

  1. It looks like a curled leaf. Light on one side, shadow on the other, a fold down the middle.
  2. Now turn it side on. If it were really curved, you'd see the bend.
  3. It's completely flat. The curve is painted on. That's the trick students take apart in Mission 001.

Inside the mission.

  1. Discover

    Hunt for hidden moths, race a detector and meet the scientists behind the research.

  2. Experiment

    Spin the wing in 3D and spot the light and shadow tricks that fake depth.

  3. Create

    Design an illusion with sliders, or write the code for it in optional coding levels.

  4. Keep

    Download a field file with what they made, what they can now do and the careers behind it.
