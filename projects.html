SQ-001

Mission 001The Illusion Engine

Some moths have flat wings that look curved. Students race a computer vision model to find camouflaged moths, spin a 3D wing, then design an illusion that fools a depth estimator.

  • Computer vision
  • 3D
  • Depth estimation
  • Years 9 to 12
  • 50 to 60 min
  • No student login
  • Ready now
Play the full mission See the teacher guide

Inside the mission.

  1. Discover

    Hunt for hidden moths, race a detector and meet the scientists behind the research.

  2. Experiment

    Spin the wing in 3D and spot the light and shadow tricks that fake depth.

  3. Create

    Design an illusion with sliders, or write the code for it in optional coding levels.

  4. Keep

    Download a field file with what they made, what they can now do and the careers behind it.
