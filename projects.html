Try this with your eyes

Is this wing curved?

  1. It looks like a curled leaf. Light on one side, shadow on the other, a fold down the middle.
  2. Now turn it side on. If it were really curved, you'd see the bend.
  3. It's completely flat. The curve is painted on. That's the trick students take apart in The Illusion Engine.

Inside The Illusion Engine.

  1. Discover

    Find camouflaged moths and compare your search with a computer vision detector.

  2. Make

    Turn a flat wing side on and design an illusion of your own with interactive controls.

  3. Test

    A depth estimator tries to read your design. See what it gets wrong.

  4. Improve

    Change one thing and test again. Optional coding challenges go deeper.

  5. Keep

    Download a Field File with your creation and the techniques behind it.
