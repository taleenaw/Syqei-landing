Privacy policy.

Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy covers the Syqei website at syqei.com. The mission app that students use has its own short student privacy notice. It has no accounts, and student work stays on the student's own device.

"Syqei", "we" and "us" means Syqei. Questions go to our privacy contact, Taleena Watts, at hello@syqei.com.

1. What we collect

Pilot requests and enquiries. Your name, email address and role, and if you choose to share them, your school or organisation, your message and whether you want updates about new missions. We also record which page you sent the form from.

Updates list. The email address you give us.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4. When you visit, it sets first-party cookies on your device and collects your device and browser type, approximate location, the pages you view, the buttons you click and how you arrived. It receives your IP address when collecting data. Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We have turned off Google Signals and advertising personalisation. We don't send names, emails or anything you type into analytics.

Hosting logs. Our host, Netlify, logs standard technical information such as IP address and browser type for security and performance.

Fonts. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

2. Why we collect it

  • To reply to your request and arrange pilots, licences or research collaborations
  • To send mission updates if you asked for them
  • To see which pages and buttons lead to pilot requests, so we can improve the site

We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

3. Who processes it and where

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores form submissions.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and Google Fonts.

Both companies may store and process data outside Australia, including in the United States.

4. How long we keep it

Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our account, which is no more than 14 months. We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and manage any pilot or licence, and review and delete old enquiries at least once a year.

5. Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing hello@syqei.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

6. Young people

This website is mainly for teachers, schools, program providers and researchers. Students usually take part through their school. If you are under 15, please ask a parent or carer to contact us with you. We do not ask for a student's school, address, date of birth or photo through this website. If you think a child has given us information here, tell us and we'll delete it.

Before a mission runs with students, we agree supervision, consent and data arrangements with the school or organisation.

7. Changes

We'll update this policy as Syqei grows and show the date of the latest change at the top of this page.
