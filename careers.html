Careers library

Who does this for a job?

Every Syqei mission ends with the real careers that use what students just did. This library grows with each mission. Only some of these jobs are mostly code.

From SQ-001. The Illusion Engine

Four careers behind one moth.

A moth that fools computers sits where biology, physics, art and code meet. So do these jobs.

C-001.1

Computer vision engineer

CodeMaths

Builds the systems that let phones, cars and drones understand images.

Where they work
Tech companies, robotics, self-driving cars, hospitals.
In Mission 001
You raced a detector built the way these engineers build theirs.
School subjects that help
  • Digital Technologies
  • Maths
  • Physics
One way to get there
Computer Science, Software Engineering or Data Science

C-001.2

Behavioural ecologist

ScienceFieldwork

Studies how animals survive, hide and fool predators. Dr Jennifer Kelley does this at UWA.

Where they work
Universities, museums, conservation groups, government.
In Mission 001
You hunted for camouflaged moths, the same question this research asks.
School subjects that help
  • Biology
  • Science
  • Maths
One way to get there
Science, majoring in Zoology, Ecology or Biology

C-001.3

Biophotonics researcher

ScienceLab

Works out how living things make colour and light. Dr Annie Jessop does this at the University of Salzburg.

Where they work
Universities, materials and sensor companies.
In Mission 001
You spun the wing and saw how light and shadow fake a shape.
School subjects that help
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
One way to get there
Science, majoring in Physics, Chemistry or Biology

C-001.4

Technical artist

ArtCode

Uses light, shadow and code to make flat screens look 3D.

Where they work
Games, film and visual effects studios.
In Mission 001
You designed an illusion that fooled a depth estimator.
School subjects that help
  • Visual Art
  • Digital Technologies
  • Maths
One way to get there
Games Design, Computer Graphics, or Art with coding

Study paths are examples, not the only way in. Plenty of people arrive at these jobs from somewhere else entirely.

Meet two of them

Dr Jennifer Kelley studies animal camouflage. Dr Annie Jessop studies how living things make colour and light. Their research on Eudocima salaminia is the start of Mission 001, and students meet them in the mission.
