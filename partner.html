Time

One 50 to 60 minute lesson. Optional coding levels stretch it to a second lesson for fast finishers.

No student accounts, names or emails. Progress saves on the student's own device and nothing they make is uploaded.

Digital Technologies, Science, STEM electives and careers programs. It also works as an enrichment session.

Read aloud on every step, keyboard support, a non-3D option and captions on the researcher video.

Questions schools ask.

Do students need accounts or emails?

No. Students open one class link. There are no logins, and progress saves in the browser on their own device.

What devices does it work on?

Recent Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on laptops and Chromebooks. Phones and tablets work but a laptop is more comfortable. Open the device check on one student laptop to be sure.

Where does it fit in the curriculum?

It suits Digital Technologies and Science in Years 9 and 10, STEM electives and careers programs. See where it fits. We can map it to your program on request.

What data do you collect about students?

No names, emails or accounts. Nothing students make is uploaded. Read the student app privacy notice.

Can a teacher run it without you?

Yes. That's the point. The teacher guide has a minute-by-minute run sheet, questions to ask and an answer guide. Founding schools in Perth can also have us there for the first lesson.

How does payment work?

We agree a fixed quote first, then send one invoice. School purchase orders are fine.

What if it doesn't work on the day?

If something on our side stops the lesson running, we reschedule it at no cost.

Do you work with programs outside school?

Yes. Holiday programs, tutoring centres and clubs can license missions for their groups. Tell us about your program.
